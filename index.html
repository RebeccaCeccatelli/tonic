Tonic

Towards Controllable Symbolic Music Generation with Energy-Based Transformers

An Energy-Based Transformer that writes music by refining its guesses, and can be steered toward louder, longer or higher notes at generation time, compared with Llama and GPT-2 baselines.

Rebecca Ceccatelli Supervised by Prof. Dr. Roger Wattenhofer (ETH Zürich) · Prof. Joseph A. Paradiso (MIT) Advised by Lancelot Blanchard (MIT)
