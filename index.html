Tonic

Energy-Based Transformers for Symbolic Music Generation and Single- and Multi-Attribute Control

Most AI models write music by picking each next step outright. Tonic drafts it first, scores how well it fits with an energy, and improves it.

Add a musical goal to that same energy, and Tonic steers toward it: louder, longer or higher notes. We compare it with traditional models of the same size, trained on the same music.

Rebecca Ceccatelli Supervised by Prof. Dr. Roger Wattenhofer (ETH) · Prof. Joseph A. Paradiso (MIT) Advised by Lancelot Blanchard (MIT)
